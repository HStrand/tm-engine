# Drafting

## Baseline card strength — first few generations

For drafts in the first couple of generations, baseline card-strength stats from `https://api.tfmstats.com/api/combinations/baselines` (`avgEloChange` field) remain informative. See `../tm-card-evaluation/SKILL.md` for how to interpret `avgEloChange` values. Later generations are less well served by these baselines.
