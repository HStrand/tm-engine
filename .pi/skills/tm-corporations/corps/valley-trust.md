# Valley Trust (CORP21)

Starts with 37 MC. Tags: Earth. Effect: As first action, draw 3 Prelude
cards and play 1 of them. When you play a card with a science tag, you
pay 2 MC less.

Valley Trust is a strong corporation that gets an explosive start with 3 preludes. This means it can effectively rush, but it is also a flexible corp that can go for engine setups. The discount on science tags is very helpful in engine setups.

Its flexibility makes it a solid performer on all maps and in most circumstances.
