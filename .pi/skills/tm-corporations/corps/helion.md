# Helion (CORP03)

Starts with 42 MC, 3 heat production. Tags: Space. Effect: you may use
heat as MC (1:1).

Helion is a relatively weak corporation for the most part.

It thrives best in rush setups since it has built-in heat production to push the temperature track. However, the ability to use heat as MC means it flexible and can also go in an engine direction.

Heat production cards become more valuable.

Temperature-locked cards like Trees and Fish become slightly stronger in the hands of Helion. Oxygen-locked cards like Predators and Birds become slightly weaker.
