# Interplanetary Cinematics (CORP05)

Starts with 30 MC, 20 steel. Tags: Building. Effect: each time you play
an event, gain 2 MC.

Interplanetary Cinematics (IC) is a strong corporation with very good starting capital and a strong effect.

The only drawback of IC is that most of the starting capital is steel, which can sometimes be hard to spend. If IC can spend its steel early, it usually gets off to a very strong start. Without steel targets, it can be pretty bad, although IC can sometimes be playable even without steel targets, since they are quite common in base + prelude and likely to turn up in drafts.

Like Mining Guild, IC wants energy production to be able to play building cards that consume energy prod.

Advanced Alloys is very potent in the starting hand for IC.

Media Group and Optimal Aerobraking are inherently strong cards that stack well with the IC effect.

IC has an advantage on the Builder milestone.
