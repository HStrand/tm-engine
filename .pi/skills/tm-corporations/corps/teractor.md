# Teractor (CORP11)

Starts with 60 MC. Tags: Earth. Effect: Earth-tagged cards cost 3 MC
less.

Teractor has the richest starting MC of any corp, but no real built-in strategic direction. Therefore, it's flexible.

Teractor excels at making expensive plays early, like Io Mining Industries and other expensive development plays.

If the starting hand has earth tags like Acquired Company, Cartel, Nuclear Zone and others, Teractor becomes a lot stronger.
