# Point Luna (CORP19)

Starts with 38 MC, 1 titanium production. Tags: Space, Earth. Effect:
when you play an Earth tag (including this), draw 1 card.

Point Luna is the strongest corporation in the game because of its built-in card-drawing effect. When combined with early earth tags like the preludes Business Empire, UNMI Contractor and Allied Banks, or project cards like Earth Office, Acquired Company and Cartel, Point Luna can become ridiculously strong.

Point Luna is an engine corporation. It usually favors a longer game, where it can benefit from its card draw.

It thrives on all maps, but is especially potent on the "engine map" Hellas.
